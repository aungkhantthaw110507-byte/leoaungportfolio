About Me

I am an architectural designer driven by structural clarity and intentional aesthetics. My academic path is grounded in analytical thinking and rigorous problem-solving, focusing heavily on how form, light, material, and human experience intersect inside designed environments.

With a deep commitment to sustainable structures and spatial systems thinking, I build foundations to contribute meaningfully to top engineering, urban planning, and premium architectural computing programs worldwide.
